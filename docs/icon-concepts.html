Orb — App Icon Concepts

Six directions for Orb's icon, plus two alternatives for Flight's planet, drawn the way macOS 26 expects them: a full-bleed 1024 pt canvas that the system masks, a calm background, and a few front-facing Liquid Glass layers with light coming from above. Each concept is shown in all four system appearances.

What the designs follow

  • Full-bleed artwork, system mask. Every icon is drawn on a square 1024 canvas; the rounded-rectangle shape (a continuous-curvature superellipse) is applied on top, as macOS 26 does. Exported SVGs are unmasked, ready for Icon Composer.
  • Layers, not illustrations. One background plus two to four foreground layers. Use “Layers” on a concept to see the stack.
  • Liquid Glass foregrounds. Translucent fills, a specular rim on every edge (bright top-left, softer bottom-right), a sheen from above and a soft shadow underneath — the system's glass treatment, approximated.
  • Front-facing and centred. No perspective, no tilted scenes; the mark sits in the optical centre with breathing room to the mask edge.
  • Four appearances. Default, Dark (dark background, tinted foreground), Clear (everything frosted white glass over the wallpaper) and Tinted (monochrome in the user's hue).
  • No text, a single idea. Each concept stays recognisable at 16 pt in the sidebar and the menu bar.
