Orb for iPhone — Tasks, first version

A companion to Orb on the Mac

The iPhone app opens the same tasks folder over IMAP that the Mac does (todo-spec §1, §3) and is a full peer. It syncs by the same rules, writes to the same log and keeps the same invariants (§16). The first version does tasks only. Mail, chat and the reading list come later and get tabs of their own. Today is then the mixed list it is on the Mac (§4.1).

The app is meant for doing tasks and capturing them on the go. It covers what you need away from the desk: see Today, tick it off, push it out, capture a task in two seconds, and get reminded on time. Things that need the keyboard on the Mac become gestures here. Bulk import, export and the tag registry's merge rules stay on the Mac.

Design rules

  • Native first. iOS 26 throughout: a floating glass tab bar and a separate search tab, glass nav buttons, floating sheets with detents, context menus with a palette row, swipe actions, a bottom accessory, interactive widgets and notification actions. No custom chrome where the system has one.
  • One vocabulary with the Mac. Postpone (the sun) and Due (the calendar) are always these two words and these two icons. The same goes for Steps, Working on, Someday, Completed, Archived and Trash. A date reads the same in a row, in the detail, in a menu and in a notification.
  • One meaning per mark. A red date with “!” is overdue, orange “Today” is due today, ▶ is working on, the ring is step progress and the orange dashed cloud means not yet on the server. The marks are the Mac's, at touch size.
  • One accent. Orb blue marks what you can act on and what is current. Today's sun is the only warm icon. Tag colours appear only on tag names.
  • Icons follow the Mac's icon guide: an outline with one accent line, never filled. The app draws them as SF Symbols at matching weight.
